🔑 Arrival & check-in

How to find us

  1. Drive along Bruckstraße and turn into Bruckwiesenweg at the corner with the red house (restaurant Da Patrizia, opposite Bull Burgerhouse).

✨ Our tips – just for you

For families

Wilhelma – zoo & botanical garden Highlight

One of Germany's most beautiful zoos, with many animal houses and glasshouses – a great day out even when it's cold or rainy.

Stuttgart-Bad Cannstatt · approx. 15 min by car · stop “Wilhelma” (U13/U14)
F3 family leisure pool, Fellbach

Indoor pool with slides, sauna and the new Jungle World for little ones (opened summer 2026). The outdoor pool is closed in winter.

Fellbach · a few minutes by car · f3-fellbach.de
Besinnungsweg on the Kappelberg

A flat, barrier-free circular walk (approx. 4 km) through the vineyards – easy with a pushchair, with views over Fellbach.

Starts near the town hall / market square
Mercedes-Benz Museum

130 years of automotive history on nine floors – exciting for children too and perfect for rainy days.

Stuttgart-Untertürkheim · approx. 15 min · Tue–Sun 9 am–6 pm, closed on Mondays
Eating out with kids

Bull Burgerhouse (★ 4.6) is about 50 m away, Trattoria Pane e Pino (★ 4.7) about 150 m.

For couples

Wine town Fellbach: wine trail & tasting Highlight

The wine trail (approx. 4.3 km) starts at the Neue Kelter of the Fellbach winegrowers (Kappelbergstraße 48) and leads through the vines to the viewpoint “Blick über Fellbach”. On the first Saturday of every month there's an open wine tasting.

Info: i-Punkt tourist office, Marktplatz 2, phone +49 711 5851-790 (closed Wed & Sun)
Burial Chapel on the Württemberg

This neoclassical chapel above the vineyards offers one of the finest views over the Neckar valley. The chapel itself is closed from November to March – but the walk and the view are worth it all year round.

Stuttgart-Rotenberg · approx. 10 min by car
Mineralbad Leuze

Warm mineral water in indoor and outdoor pools plus sauna – wonderfully relaxing, especially in winter.

Stuttgart-Bad Cannstatt · approx. 15 min · stop “Mineralbäder”
Dinner for two

Trattoria Pane e Pino (★ 4.7), Irodion (★ 4.6, Greek, now in the historic Alte Kelter) or simply next door at Da Patrizia (★ 4.5).

Esslingen & Ludwigsburg

Esslingen with its half-timbered old town and castle (approx. 20 min), or Ludwigsburg Residential Palace, one of Germany's largest Baroque palaces (approx. 25 min, guided tours in winter).

For solo & business travellers

By S-Bahn into Stuttgart Highlight

From Fellbach station the S2 or S3 takes about 12–15 minutes to Stuttgart main station – Schlossplatz, Königstraße, the market hall and the Staatsgalerie are then within walking distance.

Stuttgart Airport & trade fair: S2/S3 without changing, approx. 40–45 min
Mercedes-Benz Museum & Porsche Museum

Two must-sees for car fans, each easily explored in 2–3 hours. Both are closed on Mondays; the Porsche Museum is also closed from 18 Jan to 1 Feb 2027 for renovation.

Mercedes: Untertürkheim · Porsche: Zuffenhausen (S-Bahn Neuwirtshaus)
Stuttgart TV Tower

The world's first TV tower built of reinforced concrete – great after work too: daily 10 am–10 pm, last entry 9:30 pm.

Stuttgart-Degerloch · approx. 25 min by car
Weekend hike to the Kernenturm

Circular hike over the Kappelberg through vineyards and forest to the lookout tower – approx. 13.7 km, around 4 hours. In winter start early (it's dark from about 5 pm) and watch out for ice in the forest.

Swabian food

Maultaschen and Kässpätzle at Wirthshaus Zum Kuckuck (★ 4.4, 747 reviews) – cosy, also when dining alone.

🚆 Getting around by bus & train

  • Bus: stop Fellbach Cannstatter Platz (a few minutes' walk) – buses to the station and more.
  • S-Bahn: Fellbach station – S2 and S3 to Stuttgart main station (approx. 12–15 min), the airport/trade fair, and towards Schorndorf or Backnang. The easiest way into Stuttgart.
  • Light rail U1: due to construction, from 13 Dec 2026 the U1 terminates at Fellbach Schwabenlandhalle. Probably from March to July 2027 replacement buses will run instead of the U1 to Bad Cannstatt.

Timetables and tickets: VVS or DB Navigator app – which also show current engineering works. The Deutschlandticket is valid on all local transport.

🚗 Stuttgart is a low-emission zone: cars need a green sticker (“Umweltplakette”).
